# General principles
> [!path] [[The Great War]] › [[Rules]]

> [!abstract] Summary
> The standard CUE code of conduct, plus event rules.

All participants are bound by CUE's standard code of conduct, plus event rules.

- Respect every participant, whatever their faction
- Follow staff instructions straight away
- No exploiting bugs, unintended mechanics or out-of-bounds areas
- Keep the historical atmosphere: this is a curated event
- No unsanctioned contact with the opposing faction during play
