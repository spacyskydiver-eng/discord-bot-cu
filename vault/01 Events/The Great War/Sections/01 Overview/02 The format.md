# The format
> [!path] [[The Great War]] › [[Overview]]

> [!abstract] Summary
> A large-scale, coordinated modded event for up to 300 players.

The event is built around realism, immersion and large-scale coordination. Two alliances face off across the European theatre, with developed military systems, period equipment and a custom ruleset.
