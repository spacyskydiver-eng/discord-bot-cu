# The setting
> [!path] [[The Great War]] › [[Overview]]

> [!abstract] Summary
> The opening months of the First World War, across 1914 Europe.

The Great War is CUE's upcoming modded Minecraft event, taking place on **17 December 2026**. It is set in the opening months of the First World War across 1914 Europe, from the [[Western Front]] to the [[Eastern Front]].

Players join one of two opposing [[Alliance|alliances]] and take part in an immersive, historically themed conflict built inside Minecraft with custom mods and bespoke systems.
